# The_Moon_and_Sixpence
Excerpt of The Moon and Sixpence.

**Chapter I**

The faculty for myth is innate in the human race.

In due course a legend arose of such circumstantiality that the wise historian would hesitate to attack it.

**Chapter II**

The moral I draw is that the writer should seek his reward in the pleasure of his work and in release from the burden of his thoughts; and, indifferent to aught else, care nothing for praise or censure, failure or success.
